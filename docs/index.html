Broken Promises: what late deliveries cost a Brazilian marketplace

An analysis of 96,470 delivered orders from Olist (Sept 2016 to Aug 2018). Customers do not grade how long they wait. They grade whether the order showed up by the date they were promised. This page shows where the promise breaks and what to fix first.

What I tested that did not work

  • Lateness is mostly on the carrier side. Late orders spent about 28 days with the carrier, compared with 8 days for on-time orders. The seller side added about 3 extra days.
  • Limits: this is observational data, so the review effect is a strong association with controls, not a controlled experiment. The seller handoff estimate assumes carrier time would not change if the seller shipped on time.
